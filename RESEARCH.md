# Reaction case-study selection — 16 September 2026

Screened 22 candidates before selecting 12. Chemistry, process concepts and educational accounting are separate evidence layers. No paired experimental data or experimental operating conditions are claimed for the application datasets. Publisher abstracts, IUBMB records, primary research and official education/award summaries informed selection; some publisher full texts returned access errors. General green-chemistry principles alone were not treated as evidence that an arbitrary yield or temperature improvement will work.

Scores below are editorial website-suitability judgements, NOT chemical Green Scores: seven dimensions, each 0–2: educational relevance / green concept / representable data / metrics / optimization / module coverage / understandability. A high score does not establish practical performance or safety. Selection also considers diversity, scope and redundant examples.

## Accounting and selection limits

All 12 selected cases support atom economy, yield-input accounting, RME, E-factor and PMI, plus the same four-category educational score and thermal proxy. A mathematically valid scenario is not a kinetic or process-feasibility prediction. Existing 10 mmol assumptions are retained for the newer records; route alternatives are rescaled to equal theoretical target output. No actual mass is filled into an unmeasured coproduct row; aggregate non-target output follows mass conservation.

The final selection emphasizes aqueous/biocatalytic systems, polymer circularity and simple inorganic route comparisons. It does not claim coverage of validated solvent-free synthesis, microwave energy savings, stereoselectivity or pharmaceutical process optimization. Those need additional domain models rather than invented numeric bonuses.

Supported concept versus numerical example: source-linked immobilization does not validate the assumed per-batch enzyme allocation; high-solids research does not guarantee unchanged yield; shell feedstock does not automatically earn a greener score. Generic water/work-up/temperature plans in historical records are no longer offered by the curated picker.

## Validation

`test.mjs` exercises every selected entry through all eight tabs, original preservation, actual scenario application, mass/element balance, same-target comparison, scoring, local-storage reload, edited inputs and cross-reaction resets. Two complete passes run after fixes. `reaction-audit.json` contains the current results. DOM tests execute real application event handlers in a mock browser environment; they do not establish real-browser rendering, mobile layout or live console behavior.
